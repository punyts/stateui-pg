# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **A dataset's records are stored in its namespace** (docs/design/statenet-requests.md, R3a). Breaking. `pgWorkspaceStorage` gives `datasets`, the namespace store; `importPart` puts a data part without its records and, when the part was new, saves the records as the namespace `<owner>/data/<dataset>` at revision 1 (`PgPartRepository.put` now says whether it inserted). `txSeedDataset` does the same in an open transaction. Record embeddings follow: a dataset record's source id is `<owner>/data/<dataset>/<key>`, a namespace save queues the fields that changed (`queueNamespaceRecordChanges`), a model change or a dataset moved to another model queues every record in its namespace, `recordSource` reads the record's row and `queueAllRecords` and `RecordSimilarity` use the namespace. Tests: `src/embeddings/records.test.ts`, `src/workspace/workspaceStorage.test.ts` ("should keep an imported dataset's records in its namespace, and import it only once").
- **`PgEpochStorage` keeps a namespace's serial and requests, and lists the pending ones** (docs/design/statenet-requests.md, R2). Migration `0010_requests.sql` adds `namespace_head.serial` and `requests` and the table `pending_request` (address, id, workspace, owner, subject, at, until; no values), with row-level security by workspace. `save` writes both on the head and keeps `pending_request` in its transaction; `load` returns them; `remove` clears them. `STATE_TABLES` lists `pending_request`, so an app server with `readOnlyState` only reads it. Test: `src/state/directory.test.ts` ("should keep the serial and requests on the head, list the pending ones without values, and write a save that takes no revision").
- **`PgEpochStorage.save` writes nothing for a save that landed already under the head's epoch** (docs/design/statenet-network.md, S8). Such a save is one sent again (`isResave`: a lower revision, or the same revision and a serial no higher); before, it rewrote every row and ran `onSave` (queueing embeddings), and one with an older revision took the state back. A save at the same revision with a higher serial (a `$.server` write, a request) is written. Tests: `src/state/directory.test.ts` ("should write nothing for a save at or below the revision that landed under its epoch, and write the next one", and the serial test above).
- **`PgUiFileStore.open` reads a range of a file.** With a `ByteRange` it selects `substring(bytes from … for …)`, so only the range leaves the database, for `/paths`' byte ranges. Test: `src/workspace/workspaceStorage.test.ts` ("should open a range of a file, cut in the database, and the whole file without one").
- **`pgWorkspaceStorage` removes every persona's instance of a deleted app.** `removeApp(appId)` removes each namespace `<owner>/apps/<appId>` of the workspace whole, through the workspace's namespace store at its head's epoch (in Magnetic, the aggregate). Test: `src/workspace/workspaceStorage.test.ts` ("should remove every persona's instance of an app with removeApp, and keep the other apps'").
- **The aggregate's role lists relays in the directory, and no other node.** `migrations/0009_node_rows.sql` turns on row-level security for `statenet_node` with a policy that lets every role read and write every row as before; `grantAggregateRole` grants `INSERT`, `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on it and adds restrictive policies (`<role>_relays_insert`, `_update`, `_delete`) so that role writes only rows of `role = 'relay'`. `PgDirectory.remove` takes a node out at once. Tests: `src/db/roles.test.ts` ("should let the aggregate list, heartbeat and remove relays in the directory and no other node, while the server still writes every row"), `src/state/directory.test.ts` ("should take a node out at once with remove, and leave the others").
- **stateui-pg reports its migrations, queries, transactions, locks and part-change listener** (docs/design/reporting.md). Under `@punyts/core`'s default reporter, off unless a host turns them on: `stateui-pg.migrate` the migration lock taken, the pending count, a migration applied or failed (`owner`, `version`, `name`, `ms`); `stateui-pg.query` each query's `statement` and first `table`, `rows`, `ms` and whether it ran in a transaction, a failed one with its SQLSTATE `code` (guarded with `isReported`: off, one check per query); `stateui-pg.transaction` a transaction begun, committed (`queries`, `ms`) or rolled back (`code`), with its `workspaceIds`; `stateui-pg.lock` an advisory lock held, by the key's kind and the wait; `stateui-pg.changes` the part-change listener listening, a change heard, its connection lost and listening again. Never SQL parameters or an error's message. `@punyts/core` is a dependency now. Tests: `src/db/reporting.test.ts` ("should report migrations, queries, transactions and locks under stateui-pg.*, with names, counts and durations and no value", "should name a statement by its kind and first table, and nothing of its values").
